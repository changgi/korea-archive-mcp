요약Miserable weather conditions fail to dampen the spirits of the 1st Battalion, The Royal Australian Regiment (1RAR), as it embarks for Australia on the troopship New Australia at Inchon, after two years facing the Communists (North Koreans and Chinese) on the truce line in Korea. Here on board the landing craft most of the soldiers have eyes only for the ship which will soon take then home.
