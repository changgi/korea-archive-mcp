요약17 May 1953. Katcoms -- Koreans attached to Commonwealth units -- and Kiwis (New Zealanders) now live together with mutual regard for both sides of life. Sitting here on the steps to the Korean boys' tent are Sgmn J C Coleman (Feilding), Sigmn E J Clark (Whangarei), K Roebuck (Wanganui) and P M Robertson (Invercargill) Quantity: 1 b&w original photographic print(s).
