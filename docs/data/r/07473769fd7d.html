요약Personal reflections of Ben Wagner, A Navy Corpsman and member of the "Chosin Few". Ben served in Korea from August to December of 1950 in one of the most brutal of conditions in the Changjin or Chosin Reservoir region. Mr. Wagner spoke to the Men's Club at Dickinson Hall, The Lake Forest-Lake Bluff Senior Center in Lake Forest, Illinois
