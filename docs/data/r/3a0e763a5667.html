요약Encyclopedia Britannica (:41) presents "That War in Korea", a documentary about the Korean War of 1950 to 1953. Originally produced as a Project XX documentary. The film opens with USS Pickaway (:55). Pickaway shuttled US troops from Japan to Pusan. US soldiers bid wives and families goodbye at the start of the war (1:02). Wounded are drug across land (1:47). A funeral service follows (1:57). Rich
