요약Miserable weather failed to dampen the spirits of the 1st Battalion, The Royal Australian Regiment (1RAR) when it embarked for home on the troopship MV New Australia at Inchon. after two years facing the Communists on the truce line in Korea. Here troops are being carried out to the troopship by landing craft. Each man has their name and a number on a piece of paper stuck in their hats.
