요약The Korean War was fought between North Korea (Democratic Peoples Republic of Korea) and South Korea (Republic of Korea). North Korea had the support of China and the Soviet Union. South Korea was supported by the United Nations, primarily the United States but also New Zealand. The war began in June 1950 and ended in July 1953. It is estimated that 2 million civilians and 1 million soldiers died in this conflict.
