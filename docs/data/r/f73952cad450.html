요약Issue of China Monthly Review, January 1957, reporting on trial of J.W. Powell, his wife S.C. Powell and J. Schuman for sedition; copy of circular letter by Needham appealing for support for the Powells and Schuman, May 1957. The defendants were editors of China Monthly Review and had published the charges of the International Commission against the US.
