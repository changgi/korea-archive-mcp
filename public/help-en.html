🗣 Multilingual queriesAsk in Korean or English — queries auto-convert to each index language (Corée for Gallica), and period spellings (Corea·Chosen·長津湖) are preserved, not "corrected".
Spelling variants in parallelKorea·Corea·Corée·朝鮮 — period spelling changes the result set

8. FAQ

I got zero hits.

Zero is not absence. Try period spellings (Corea·Chosen), adjacent mining, or switch to “the administration that governed the object” — the server suggests these automatically.

Does it cover the Joseon dynasty?

Yes — statute search, sibling-clause sweeps, roster filtering, and Seungjeongwon Ilgi date verification are built in. Try “조선의 ○○ 제도 풀패키지로”.

Can I get the ZIP package?

ZIP delivery is local (Claude Code). On web/mobile, deliverables arrive as individual artifacts with a manifest.

Which archives are searched?

US NARA, UK TNA, Internet Archive, Gallica (BnF), Europeana, and nine Korean institutions including the Korean History DB, Kyujanggak, and KOREAN WAR ARCHIVES (MOU partner).
